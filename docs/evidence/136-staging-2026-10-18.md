José explicitly requested scenario 1 (the reason for Unit of Work), rather than
accepting deferral. The previously stated need for special failure injection was
incorrect: inspection of the deployed source identifies a natural post-insert
failure path already present in the normal application.

Unit of Work, locks the Bank row, awaits `expenses.CreateAsync`, then calls
`LinkAsync`. The EUR eligibility predicate is in that **later guarded UPDATE**;
zero rows throws 409 and disposal rolls back the Unit of Work. The source at
observed deployed commit `ea41d9c918623ef6f973dc416194bd612ca5aa10` has no diff
from the inspected implementation. An unknown category is an earlier rejection
and is not this experiment.

Transaction **1224** was still the exact fake USD account row, needsReview/null
link. One POST to `/BankTransaction/1224/create-and-match` submitted valid EUR

constraint bypass or cleanup was used.
